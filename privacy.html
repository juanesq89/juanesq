4. Your consent, and your right to refuse it

Before the inquiry form can be sent, you must tick the consent box. Consent is entirely voluntary and you may refuse it.

If you refuse: we cannot receive your inquiry through this form, and the form will not send. Nothing else on this site is affected — you can still read every guide, and you can still reach the office directly by e-mail at wwhong@snkpartners.com or by WhatsApp, neither of which requires consent through this website.

5. Your rights

At any time you may ask us to:

  • let you see the personal information we hold about you (access);
  • correct it if it is wrong (correction);
  • delete it (deletion);
  • stop processing it (suspension of processing).

Send the request to wwhong@snkpartners.com. We will act on it without delay and reply within 10 days. You may make the request through a representative, in which case we will ask for a letter of authority. We will not treat you unfavourably for exercising any of these rights.

One limit: where information has become part of a case file that we are required to retain, we may not be able to delete it before that retention period ends. If that applies, we will tell you why.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you may contact the Korea Internet & Security Agency Privacy Call Centre (dial 118 in Korea, privacy.kisa.or.kr) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr).

6. Destruction

When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we destroy the information without delay — electronic files by a method that cannot be recovered, and any printed copy by shredding or incineration.

7. Keeping it safe

Access to inquiries is limited to the attorney and staff handling the matter. Traffic to and from this site is encrypted (HTTPS). Independently of this policy, anything you tell us is also covered by the duty of confidentiality that the Attorney-at-Law Act imposes on us.

8. Changes to this policy

Any change is posted on this page. Where the change is material, we will note it here before it takes effect.

Effective 13 September 2026.
