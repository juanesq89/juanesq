About · Juan W. Hong, Attorney at Law

Juan W. Hong.
Korean attorney, four languages.

Before law I worked in ship sales at Hyundai Heavy Industries, on the Latin America desk at POSCO International covering renewable energy, and as a Spanish interpreter at Samsung SDS. I know what it is to deal with an unfamiliar system in someone else's country, and to negotiate when the other side sets the terms and the language.

I practise at SNK Law Firm in Seoul. Matters are handled by the firm; I am the person you talk to, in your language, from the first message onward.
