Consultations

Book a consultation.
Paid, scheduled, in your language.

A consultation is a set time with me to go through your situation properly — what the law says, what your options are and what I would do next. It is paid and booked in advance, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Prices include VAT. If you instruct me on the matter, the consultation fee is credited toward the retainer.

How it works

  1. 01Choose a time

    Pick a free slot in my calendar. For an in-person meeting, give two or three times that suit you.

  2. 02Payment details by e-mail

    I reply within one business day to confirm a time and send payment details.

  3. 03Confirmed once paid

    The booking is confirmed when payment arrives. A time left unpaid for 24 hours is released.

Before the consultation

Once your booking is confirmed, please send me the full story and every document you have by e-mail, at least one business day before we speak. The more I have read beforehand, the more of our time goes to answers rather than questions — and the more precise those answers can be.

  • A timeline of what happened, in your own words
  • Contracts, notices, letters and anything you have signed
  • Court, police, prosecution or immigration papers
  • Messages, e-mails and receipts that matter to the story
  • Your residence card or visa details, if your status is involved
