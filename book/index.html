Consultations

Book a consultation.
Paid, scheduled, in your language.

A consultation is a set time with me to go through your situation properly — what the law says, what your options are and what I would do next. It is paid and booked in advance, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Prices include VAT. If you instruct me on the matter, the consultation fee is credited toward the retainer.

How it works

  1. 01Request a time

    Choose the type of consultation and give two or three times that suit you.

  2. 02Payment details by e-mail

    I reply within one business day to confirm a time and send payment details.

  3. 03Confirmed once paid

    The booking is confirmed when payment arrives. A time left unpaid for 24 hours is released.
